Practice 1: Completeness and Relevance Evaluation

Objective

Evaluate AI-generated responses based on completeness, relevance, and compliance with the user’s instructions.

User Prompt

“What are three benefits of regular exercise? Keep your answer brief and suitable for a 12-year-old.”

Response A

“Regular exercise can strengthen your muscles and bones, improve heart health, and support mental well-being.”

Response B

“Exercise is important for staying healthy. It can strengthen your muscles and bones. It can also improve heart health. Regular exercise may support mental well-being. Exercise can also help people sleep better.”

Evaluation

1. Completeness

Response A provides exactly three benefits, as requested.

Response B provides more than three benefits, adding sleep as another benefit beyond the requested number.

2. Relevance

Both responses are relevant because they explain benefits of regular exercise.

3. Instruction Following

Response A follows the request to provide three benefits and keep the answer brief.

Response B includes additional information and does not follow the requested number of benefits as precisely.

4. Final Ranking

Response A ranks higher.

It answers the user’s question with exactly three benefits and follows the brevity instruction.

Key Learning

A response is not automatically better because it contains more information. Completeness means providing all the information requested, not adding unnecessary details.

Evaluator Feedback

“Response A ranks higher because it provides exactly three relevant benefits and follows the user’s instruction to keep the answer brief. Response B is relevant but includes an additional benefit that was not requested.”
