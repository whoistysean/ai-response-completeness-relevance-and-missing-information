Practice 2: Identifying Missing Information

Objective

Practise identifying missing information in AI-generated responses and distinguishing relevant answers from complete answers.

User Prompt

“Explain three ways regular exercise benefits the human body. Include one physical benefit, one mental benefit, and one benefit related to sleep.”

Response A

“Regular exercise strengthens muscles and improves physical fitness. It can also support mental well-being by helping some people manage stress.”

Response B

“Regular exercise strengthens muscles and improves physical fitness. It may support mental well-being and help some people manage stress. Regular physical activity can also improve sleep quality for some people.”

Evaluation

1. Completeness

Response A is incomplete because it covers physical and mental benefits but does not include the requested sleep-related benefit.

Response B is more complete because it addresses all three requested categories.

2. Relevance

Both responses are relevant because they explain ways regular exercise benefits the human body.

3. Missing Information

The specific information missing from Response A is a benefit related to sleep.

4. Final Ranking

Response B ranks higher.

It includes a physical benefit, a mental benefit, and a sleep-related benefit, satisfying all three content requirements.

Key Learning

A response can be relevant without being complete. Evaluators should identify the exact information missing rather than simply describing an answer as poor.

Evaluator Feedback

“Response B ranks higher because it addresses all three requested benefit categories: physical fitness, mental well-being, and sleep quality. Response A is relevant but incomplete because it omits the requested sleep-related benefit.”
