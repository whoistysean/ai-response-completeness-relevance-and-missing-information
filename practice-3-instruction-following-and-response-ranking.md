Practice 3: Instruction Following and Response Ranking

Objective

Evaluate AI-generated responses by comparing completeness, relevance, and instruction following while identifying unnecessary information.

User Prompt

“Explain three benefits of eating fruits regularly. Include one benefit related to heart health, one related to digestion, and one related to the immune system. Keep your answer simple and brief.”

Response A

“Eating fruits regularly provides vitamins and minerals that support the immune system. Fruits also contain fibre, which helps digestion. Some fruits contain nutrients that support heart health.”

Response B

“Eating fruits regularly can support heart health, improve digestion, and strengthen the immune system. Fruits are also rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and fibre. Eating a variety of fruits can contribute to a balanced diet and overall health.”

Evaluation

1. Completeness

Both responses are complete because they address all three requested benefit categories:

* Heart health
* Digestion
* Immune system

2. Relevance

Both responses are relevant because they directly explain benefits of eating fruits regularly.

3. Instruction Following

Response A follows the instruction to keep the answer simple and brief.

Response B includes additional nutritional information that was not specifically requested, making it less concise.

4. Final Ranking

Response A ranks higher.

It provides all three requested benefits while following the user’s instruction to keep the answer simple and brief.

Key Learning

More information does not automatically make an AI response better. A strong response should provide the requested information without unnecessary additions.

Evaluator Feedback

“Both responses are relevant and complete because they address all three requested benefit categories. However, Response A ranks higher because it communicates the required information more concisely and follows the user’s instruction to keep the answer simple and brief.”
